Elixir Fit · Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

In short

Your data stays on your phone and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. The app's author has no servers, doesn't receive your data and can't see it.

What data the app stores

Only what you log: weight, measurements, meals, water, steps, workouts, injections, blood pressure, sleep, goals and the data used to calculate calories (sex, age, height). This can include health information, so treat it with care.

Where it is stored

  • On your device: in the browser/installed app storage. Progress photos stay only here and are never sent anywhere.
  • In your Google Drive (optional): if you tap "Connect Google Drive", the app saves a file in a private, hidden app folder (drive.appdata permission). The app can't see your other files, and each person has their own copy in their own account.

Third-party services

  • Google: to sign in and save the file in your Drive, only if you turn on sync.
  • Open Food Facts: when you scan a barcode, the code number is sent to this public database to get the product name and nutrition values.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app files. Like any website, it may log the IP address of visitors, according to GitHub's policy.

The app has no ads and uses no analytics or tracking cookies.

How to delete your data

  • In More → Profile → Disconnect Google Drive the app stops syncing and loses access.
  • To delete the file in Drive: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Elixir Fit → Delete hidden app data.
  • To delete the data on your phone, remove the app from the home screen and clear the site data in Safari settings.

Important notice

The app is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Calories, protein, water and burn are general estimates. Always talk to your doctor or dietitian before making health decisions, especially if you take medication.
